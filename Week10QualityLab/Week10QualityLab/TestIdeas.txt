Activity 3.3 review questions

Did Copilot suggest any unclear tests?
  Yes. The rounding test (Idea 10) and the negative shipping test (Idea 11) depend on
  rules nobody has stated. Idea 14 is unclear because it does not fit the C# type
  system. These need a decision from the product owner before being trusted.

Did Copilot miss any important boundary cases?
  Yes, typically:
  - Free shipping is decided on the DISCOUNTED total, so the important combined case
    is a price that only reaches exactly $100 after discount (e.g. 125 with 20% = 100).
  - Just above the threshold (100.01).
  - Price of 0.
  - Discounts at the edges combined with shipping (100% discount => total is only shipping).
  I added some of these myself (see the "Optional extras" tests in OrderServiceTests.cs).

Which tests are based on real requirements?
  Ideas 1-9: the complaint ($200 at 20% should be $160), the stated rule ("free
  shipping at $100 or more"), and sensible percentage limits (0 to 100).

Which tests are based only on the current code?
  Ideas 11, 13 and 14, and partly 10 and 12. They describe what the code happens to do
  or what its structure suggests, not what the business asked for. Idea 13 is the
  clearest danger: it would make the bug "correct".

Key reminder: Copilot can suggest useful tests, but a human must check each one
against the actual requirements.
